## Method, labels, and count caveat

TechCrunch supplies the cohort name, URL, and category but no company descriptions. On 2026-08-23, each listed URL was first attempted with redirects and a 15-second timeout; page title, description, headings, and visible copy were reviewed. Stage C then revisited every failed URL and every thin page named in the uncertainty register in a full Chrome renderer, recovering 14 failed sites and materially improving four thin entries. The resulting depth is **200 indexed; 196 researched; 4 retained as unresolved stubs**. “Explicit” AI means the rendered company copy used an AI/ML/agent/predictive or equivalent mechanism. “No explicit claim” does not prove no AI is used.

Support labels are analytical: **DIRECT** = customer support/CX/onboarding product; **ADJACENT** = enterprise workflow, agent, communications, or knowledge layer next to support; **PATTERN** = a transferable mechanic such as routing, escalation, sentiment, memory, evaluation, trust, or orchestration; **NONE** = no material support connection evidenced.

**Category terminology and discrepancy:** This file uses **short normalized labels** (for example, “Enterprise tech” and “Biotech”) rather than reproducing TechCrunch's longer category headings verbatim. Step 5 says “21 category headings,” but both its enumerated distribution and TechCrunch’s canonical article contain **20** headings. The 20 stated numbers sum to exactly 200; inventing a twenty-first would make the source and counts false.

## Transferable patterns worth stealing

1. **Prevent the ticket, not just answer it.** GuideAI observes where users stall and intervenes in-product; ChargeMate combines driver help with visibility into what is breaking. Detect friction from product events and act before a complaint.
2. **One case memory across channels.** Pocodot spans messaging, email, and phone; Dosu captures shared knowledge. Use a channel-independent case object with durable evidence and handoff state.
3. **Simulate and red-team before exposure.** Marketrix simulates users, Circuit Breaker Labs pressure-tests conversations, and Laplace rehearses what-ifs. Build synthetic personas, adversarial cases, and replayable evaluations.
4. **Govern every action, not only every answer.** HODOR scopes tools and logs calls; Trinitite adds guards, tests, memory, and proof; Tzun records oversight. Expose policy checks, permissions, confidence, evidence, and approvals.
5. **Escalation is a designed outcome.** Care Mojo verifies risk before alerting people; LumenUs combines software with human help; Atreus coordinates constrained recovery. Select the right human and hand over context, not merely a chat transcript.
6. **Domain experts become executable knowledge.** Expert Scale captures practitioner knowledge and Collar turns company evidence into workflow-owning agents. Pair retrieval with executable playbooks, provenance, and expert review.
7. **Use signals beyond text sentiment.** Virtuosis derives voice biomarkers, Motion Sync predicts discomfort, and Cautio raises event alerts. Combine words, acoustic cues, recurrence, and account events without treating emotion inference as certain.
8. **Close resolution back to root cause.** Niobia correlates process and quality data; ChargeMate exposes recurring breakage. Resolved cases should update issue clusters, product telemetry, knowledge gaps, and prevention actions.

## Crowded spaces to avoid

- **Generic AI copilots.** Ariso, Honeyjar, Kilo, Pocodot, AskMAE, and others already occupy horizontal assistant language. A buildathon entry needs a sharply bounded support decision or workflow.
- **Undifferentiated autonomous agents.** Bounty, Collar, Destro, onSpark, Watad, and YOUnifiedAI promise end-to-end action. Defensibility must come from support-specific state, policy, evaluation, or data.
- **Basic RAG answers.** Dosu and Expert Scale already make organizational knowledge available. Retrieval alone will not demonstrate originality or AI depth.
- **Generic sentiment dashboards.** Aiphrodite, GUDEA, Virtuosis, and NTangible show richer behavior, narrative, voice, and performance signals. Positive/negative classification is behind the cohort.
- **Voice bots without assurance.** Apate, DetectifAI, GuideAI, Care Mojo, and Your360 use voice, while Circuit Breaker Labs and Straiker show why tests and controls matter. Voice is a modality, not differentiation.
- **Generic AI governance.** Cude, Hardshell, HODOR, Straiker, Trinitite, Tzun, and Verapath crowd control-plane tooling. Apply governance to concrete support harm and escalation decisions.
